Project overview

This work explores how digital behavior is described as registers, datapaths and finite-state machines, verified in simulation and exercised on physical hardware. I used the Artix-7-based Cmod A7 board and Vivado to build progressively more complex logic and test the relationship between RTL behavior and physical device timing.

SPI display bring-up

The TFT controller accepts serial clock and data signals along with chip-select, data/command, reset and backlight controls. The initial hardware objective was deliberately small: initialize the display and draw two rectangular Pong-style paddles.

The first display test successfully drew the bars. Their initial orientation was rotated relative to the intended layout, providing a practical next step for display memory addressing and rotation settings.
