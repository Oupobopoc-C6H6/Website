About Me

Professional Background

I earned my B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from the University of South Florida in 2026 and am pursuing an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering, with a focus on bioelectronics and analytical engineering. My work combines hands-on electronics development, biomedical instrumentation, and experimental testing.

Through engineering design projects and research, I've worked on microcontroller-based systems, electronic prototyping, sensor integration, signal analysis, and device validation. I also serve as a graduate assistant supporting engineering capstone design.

Engineering Interests

Although my education is in biomedical engineering, I'm interested in applying my technical skills across industries, particularly in:

  • Embedded systems and electronics: Microcontroller programming, circuit design, PCB layout, and hardware integration.
  • Low-power design: Battery-powered electronics, power management, and efficient device operation.
  • Signal processing and instrumentation: Sensor interfaces, data acquisition, filtering, and analysis.
  • Engineering design and testing: Prototyping, troubleshooting, and validating devices against technical requirements.

Personal Motivation

Medical technology has been part of my life from the beginning. I underwent a fetal cardiac intervention before birth and later received an On-X mechanical aortic valve. Those experiences helped inspire my interest in biomedical engineering and gave me a patient-centered perspective on the importance of dependable, thoughtful device design.

That motivation continues to shape my approach to engineering, whether I'm working on healthcare technology or electronic systems with applications beyond medicine.
